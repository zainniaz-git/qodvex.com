Printing and testing guide

Keep strong contrast between dark modules and a light background, leave the quiet-zone margin intact, and print no smaller than about 2 cm wide. Scan the final printed copy with at least two phones before distributing it.

FAQ

Is Qodvex free?

Yes. You can generate and download QR codes without an account.

Is my data uploaded?

No. The QR code is built in your browser, and your content is not sent to a server by this tool.

Will my QR code expire?

No. The code stores your content directly, so it works as long as the destination (such as a website) still exists.

Which error correction level should I use?

M suits most uses. Choose Q or H for printed codes that may get scuffed, keeping in mind the code becomes denser.

Why won't my QR code scan?

Check contrast, margin, size and whether the content is very long. Dark foreground on a light background scans best.
